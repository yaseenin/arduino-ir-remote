Method A: Direct (Short Range ~0.5m)

Connect Anode (+) of IR LED to Pin 3 through 220Ω resistor. Connect Cathode (-) directly to GND.

Method B: Transistor Boost (Full Range ~5-8m)

Use 2N2222 NPN. Base to Pin 3 (220Ω), Collector to IR LED Cathode (-), Anode to 5V (100Ω), Emitter to GND.
